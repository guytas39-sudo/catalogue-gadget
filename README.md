A studio catalogue photograph of the **TOAST & PRESS 3000** — a chrome toaster that toasts your bread and irons your shirt at the same time. A white dress shirt is draped over the toaster, being pressed, while two slices pop up golden; a necktie lies beside it, freshly pressed and ready.

## How it is made

`draw_image.py` draws `entry.png` (1024×1024) with Pillow — chrome gradient body, tilted toast, a draped shirt with collar and placket, steam, and one accessory (the tie). `main.py` (the entry program, Python 3.13 stdlib only) copies `entry.png` to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.png`, so both runs write identical bytes. Deterministic: re-running `draw_image.py` produces the same sha256.
